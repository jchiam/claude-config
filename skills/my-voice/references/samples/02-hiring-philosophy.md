# Why do we need a Recruitment Philosophy?

Recruitment philosophy serves as the northstar in guiding the organisation, hiring managers and recruitment function on the people we would hire, and how to hire them. Hiring managers will use the principles here to guide their hiring plans and decisions, by understanding the type of people we are looking for, and enables the recruitment function to better guide the division in coming up with recruitment processes and strategies that could lead to better success in hiring the right people.

The recruitment philosophy should be endorsed by the Leadership Team for concurrence and alignment.

# Hire the Right People

While we value diversity in people (don't hire 11 quarterbacks!!), hiring the right people based on characteristics that align with the division's values is important.

**Potential to move the needle**

We welcome candidates with the potential to help the division achieve her vision (*"To make technology work for everyone it serves."*) in whatever capacity possible.

**Capabilities that meet team's needs, or potential to be groomed**

We evaluate the candidate to meet the division's needs. While the current capabilities of the candidate are important, we also take into account the potential for the candidate to be groomed to fully meet the needs in reasonable time.

**Positive influence to growth of others**

The candidate should have positive influence to the growth of others. E.g. being a role model, mentor, subject matter expert, leader.

# Hire with the Right Mindset

The division is a competitive choice for potential candidates, with the following key offerings in mind.

**Growth opportunities**

We provide growth opportunities for our staff to stretch their capabilities and achieve their personal and career goals.

**Coaching and mentorship**

Managers, functional leaders and peers work together to provide coaching and mentorship to support staff growth and success.

**Flat organisation**

The division endorses the culture of self-managing cross-functional teams, where staff are granted the autonomy to work with various individuals to achieve business goals and create impact.

# Hire with the Right Way

We hire with alignment to recruitment goals.

**Fair and consistent hiring**

We strive to eliminate bias and ensure consistency in the interview process. Interviewers should make independent assessments. Similarly, the hiring manager should make independent decisions in the recommendation for hire.

**Provide a great candidate experience, leave a positive impression of the division**

The personnel involved in the interview process represent the division to potential candidates. Leave a good impression!
