The current product direction is to develop a **common platform that benefits the organisation at the organisational level**. Given the team's experiences with Product A and Product B, a few insights can be made.

1. Branch X is a branch of domain experts in their field and has very bespoke requirements for Product A
2. Branch Y is always open to purchasing off-the-shelf SaaS if said products are available and compliant
3. Branch Y seems to be the only branch from HQ that runs such exercises for the staff population

# Product A

Product A is **not** inline with the product direction as it is a bespoke system that Branch X needs to run its exercise. It has many bespoke features, e.g. on-site invigilation, that only apply to Product A. As such, after the current cycle, the division will engage Branch X in the following manner.

- Product A is no longer a priority for the division's resources
- The division will suggest for Branch X to fund augmented manpower resources should development be required (TBC)
- The division will still dedicate resources to support Product A operationally

# Product B

The division will no longer support Branch Y with bespoke requirements that are not inline with the goal of a common platform. One of the major features to be removed is analysis and report generation as they were engineered to be entirely bespoke to automate Branch Y's report generation requirements and are not scalable. However, feedback from Branch Y should still be taken seriously as they will be the major stakeholder of the common platform. Analysis and report generation will be handled by Branch Y through upskilling and support with established tools on the market such as Tableau. This support and upskilling is part of the data upskilling initiative.

Product B will provide the foundation to pivot towards a common platform.

# Product C

Product C, also run by Branch Y, will follow the same roadmap as Product B. The short-term development plans would be to explore ways to manage the development of the system in a sane manner.
