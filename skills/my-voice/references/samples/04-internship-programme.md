While teams benefit from interns' presence and contributions, making it our mission to provide great experiences for our interns could have positive impact such as:

- Potential conversions to full time of interns
- Raise the division's reputation in the fresh graduate job market through word of mouth

This page documents some aspects and initiatives to be considered and executed for the summer interns this year.

# Onboarding

## Application of Staff/Temp passes

For internships less than 3 months, temp passes will be issued. For internships lasting for 3 months and more, staff passes will be issued.

For this round, @Lead A consolidated the requests on behalf of the division to save duplicated efforts and to streamline the requests for the admin team, in accordance to the discussion.

The RO/buddy will have to arrange time with the admin team and bring the intern down on their first day for the passes.

## Laptops

There is a need to provision laptops to interns for use, should they choose to. There is no mandate at the moment that interns cannot use their personal laptops.

For roles where access to services through managed devices is required, ROs may consider advising interns against using their personal laptops.

For this round, @Lead B has informed and is working with Finance function to ensure there are sufficient laptops for interns now and in future.

## Buddies for Interns

Similar to how new hires have a designated buddy to support onboarding processes, there could be benefits in assigning one for interns as well. Having discussed with @Lead C and @Lead B from Workplace function, most of the onboarding efforts would have been consolidated upfront. There is no clear need for a designated buddy outside of the team to support at the moment. This can be explored further when the need arises.

## Access to Division Services

Existing access granting for interns starts from the baseline interns LDAP group with minimal access. ROs will then need to request for specific access on a needs basis to Workplace function.

# Aspects of a Holistic Intern Experience

## Inclusiveness

### Department Offsite (May)

All the interns, except one, will be starting before the Department Offsite. It could be beneficial for interns to partake in it for insights to the larger organisation.

### Division Anniversary (July)

Interns should be included by default. No additional action items.

## Value-add

### Recommend frequent 1:1 with ROs

Just as full-time staff get regular engagement through 1:1 with their ROs, there should be similar opportunities set up for regular check-ins to ensure interns are getting fair treatment and are feeling engaged.

### [Proposal] Engagement session with the Director

This can be a one-off session, considering the internship is 3 months for most, where the Director is made available to engage with the interns. The exact format can be deliberated. Intent is to provide an opportunity for interns to speak to the Director, to understand more about the wider organisational landscape and the part the division plays. Ultimately, we can also take this chance to sell koyok.

## Recognition

### Intern Showcase

In order to recognise our interns' contributions and efforts, the intern showcase can be organised as a sharing session, possibly during one of the huddles. The exact format is to be confirmed. Support from ROs will be required as we would expect the interns to be given at least one properly scoped out piece of work, or gain exposure in ways that contribute to their learning. ROs should also guide the interns in their work leading up to the showcase.

## Feedback

Feedback was solicited from departing interns to understand their engagement during their stay in the division, and to explore possible improvements to the programme for subsequent batches of interns.

A survey was conducted to gather inputs specifically on:

- Whether the internship experience was beneficial
- Efficacy of ROs/Leads
- Sentiments on specific initiatives (e.g. engagement with the Director and intern showcase)
- Whether intern will consider recommending the division to others
- General feedback
