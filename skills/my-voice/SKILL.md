# My Voice

Produce writing that reads as if Jonathan wrote it, not as polished AI prose.

## Before writing

1. Read `references/style-guide.md` in full.
2. Read the samples in `references/samples/` closest to the requested genre (at least two):
   - Strategy / proposal → `01-strategy-proposal.md`, `03-engineering-assessment.md`
   - Principles / philosophy / guidelines → `02-hiring-philosophy.md`
   - Programme or process plan → `04-internship-programme.md`
   - Stakeholder positioning / product direction → `05-product-direction.md`
3. Samples use placeholders ("the division", "Platform A", "@Lead A"). Never carry them into output; use the real names the user gives.

## Modes

- **Draft** — user gives a topic, notes or bullets. Ask for missing facts (teams, dates, numbers, decisions) rather than inventing them; specifics are core to the voice.
- **Rewrite** — user gives existing text. Preserve every fact and decision; change only voice and structure. Flag anything you had to drop.
- **Review** — user gives text and asks if it sounds like them. Quote the lines that break voice (especially section 7 AI tells), and suggest a rewrite for each.

## Self-check before returning

- No phrase from style guide section 7. No em-dashes.
- At least one named team, forum, date or number per section where facts allow.
- Bullets are plain fragments; argument lives in prose.
- British spelling.
- Did not copy typos from the samples.

Do not mention the skill, the style guide or the samples in the output itself.
